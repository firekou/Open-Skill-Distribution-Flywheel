# Trend & Material Opportunity Scoring

Score materials independently from fork eligibility.

## Material Score — 100
- Technical utility: 25
- Momentum/freshness: 20
- ATK relevance: 20
- Content potential: 15
- Experimentability: 10
- Source credibility: 10

## Routing
80–100: Priority A material
65–79: Priority B
50–64: Watch
<50: Archive

## Separate flags
These do not reduce discovery score automatically:
- license_status
- redistribution_allowed
- security_review_status
- fork_eligible

A high-scoring project with restrictive licensing can still be an excellent research/content item.

## Content Opportunity Score
For items routed to content, additionally rate 0–5:
- hook strength
- audience pain
- explainability
- novelty
- demonstrability
- ATK angle
- social portability

Never inflate technical score merely because ATK can monetize it.
