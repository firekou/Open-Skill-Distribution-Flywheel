### 1.2 Where the figures are recorded

Figures are recorded in four places and nowhere else.

* **The quarterly narrative** — sections 2 to 5. One subsection per incident, written during the
  quarter by the incident commander. The narrative gives the severity the event was *worked at* on
  the day and an approximate duration in prose.
* **The quarter incident register** — the final subsection of each quarter's section, numbered
  `2.9`, `3.9`, `4.9` and `5.9`. One table row per incident of that quarter, with the closing
  values of every recorded field. There is no combined annual register: an incident appears in the
  register of the quarter its `start_utc` falls in, and in no other.
* **Appendix A — Register Amendments** (section 7), issued 2032-01-20 after the year-end review.
* **Appendix B — Correction Notices** (section 8), issued 2032-02-14 after the external audit.

recorded at closure are frequently different, and the narrative and the register may therefore

### 1.4 Precedence of figures

Figures for the same incident appear in more than one place in this report. Where they differ,
the following precedence decides the value, highest first:

1. **Appendix B — Correction Notices.** A notice supersedes every other source in this report for
   the one incident and the one field it names.
2. **Appendix A — Register Amendments.** An amendment supersedes the quarter register for the one
   incident and the one field it names, and is itself superseded by a Correction Notice naming the
   same incident and the same field.
3. **The quarter incident register** (sections 2.9, 3.9, 4.9, 5.9).
4. **The quarterly narrative** (sections 2 to 5), which is the least authoritative source in this
   report and never decides a field that a register records.

Three further rules apply to that precedence.

**R-1. Unnamed fields are unchanged.** A field of an incident that is named by neither an amendment
nor a correction notice keeps the value its quarter register records.

**R-2. Withdrawal.** An amendment whose `field` is `withdrawn` removes the incident from the
register of this reporting year in full. A withdrawn incident is not an incident of 2031: it must
not appear in any table derived from this report, whatever severity it carries and whatever any
other source says about it. No correction notice reinstates a withdrawn incident.

**R-3. Selection happens last.** Any selection of incidents by severity, by root cause or by
customer impact is made **after** every amendment and every correction notice has been applied.
An incident may enter such a selection, or leave it, as a result.
